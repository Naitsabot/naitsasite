# I Looove Pancakes 🥞

This recipe is very easy, and I stole it from my mother at some point.

## Ingredients

Servings: IDK, 4-8 pancakes? What is a serving of pancakes?

- 2 eggs
- 350 ml or g of milk (I usually use 1.5% milk)
- 200 g wheat flour (or something else, just adjust the amount)
- Some vanilla sugar (like a teaspoon)
- Olive oil, palm oil or unsalted butter
- Stuff to put on/in the pancake

## Preparation

- Whisk eggs in a bowl
- Whisk with milk
- Add everything else dry in (aim for the batter to be runny, but not water-runny)
- Heat a pan on the stove on medium-high, just enough that your oily substance does not burn
- Scoop batter onto the pan, and spread it by turning
- Wait a bit
- Turn the pancake when you can see the bottom under the pancake beginning to brown
- Wait a bit
- When the other side becomes somewhat done, take it out of the pan
- Eat it with something
- Repeat
