# I Loooove Pancakes 🥞

This recipe is very easy, and I stole it from my mother at some point.

## Ingredients

Servings: IDK, 4-8 pancakes? What is a serving of pancakes?

- 2 eggs
- 350 ml or g of milk (i usually use 1,5% milk)
- 200 g wheat flour (or somwething else, just adjust the amount)
- Some vanilla sugar (like a teaspoon)
- Olive oil, palmin or unsalted butter
- Stuff to put on/in the pancake

## Preparation

- Wisk eggs in bowl
- Wisk with milk
- Throw everything else dry in (aim for the batter to be runny, but not water-runny-ish)
- Heat a pan on the stove on medium-high, just enought the that the your oily substance does not burn
- Scoope batter onto pand, and spread it by turning
- Wait a bit
- Turn the pancake when you can see the bottom under the pancake begins browning
- Wait a bit
- Can the otehr side becomes somewhat done take out of pan
- Eat it with something
- Repeat
